Each one will show the event, the sector it sits in, and what it opens for SSG.

Situations group the stories of one deal, round, listing or process across the weeks it runs, by the company they are about (read from the headline) and by shared words, so a dozen headlines on one sale process read as one card. The phase is the furthest any report got: Reported (explores, weighs, in talks), In process (advisers hired, data room, bids, an unaccepted offer), Agreed, Completed. The heat is the hottest story's. A card is shown when a situation has more than one story or is in process; tap it for every story.

Priority is ordered by Heat, a 0–100 score for the Singapore SSG team: APAC relevance (25 — Singapore and SE Asia first, then India and ANZ, then North Asia), live process (20), technology intensity (20), sponsor relationship (15), deal size (10) and ten for the day it breaks; from then on the whole number fades with age — full for two days, two-thirds at a week, half at ten days — so the list always leads with the latest. Tap the flame number for the breakdown. An item is on the list when it is relevant: an APAC item whenever it carries an event or a sector; a global item when it is a transaction — a sale process, a deal or a listing in one of SSG's sectors, a growth round of $100m or more, anything with a billion-dollar figure — or a platform shift that reaches a sector. The rest is kept behind the link at the bottom; the page carries the last 90 days and the archive in the repo keeps everything. Event, sector and APAC are read from the headline by keyword; the matched words sit under each tag, and the small label is the beat the story came from. A story tagged BACKGROUND is a profile page or a re-told old deal and sits below the bar. Pursue, Watch, Dismiss and notes are saved and shared with anyone who can open this page.
