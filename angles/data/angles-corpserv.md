## 4. Vistra: a technology equity story for EQT eleven years in

- **Why now:** EQT's Asian franchise has held Vistra since 2015 and merged it with Tricor at a US$6.5bn enterprise value in July 2023; it added a fund-administration business and launched Vistra Fund Solutions in April 2025. [EQT, 28 Jul 23](https://eqtgroup.com/news/bpea-eqt-completes-the-merger-of-vistra-and-tricor-creating-a-global-fund-and-corporate-services-platform) [AAP / PR Newswire, 7 Apr 25](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20250407ae58816)

- **The pitch to EQT:** an outside-in technology assessment of the merged entity-management, fund-administration and client-portal stack, as the first chapter of a vendor report.
- **Why SSG:** Product and Tech DD on a 9,000-person services platform, where the technology decides the margin a buyer will underwrite.

## 5. Payroll Inc.: a cloud and automation roadmap for TA Associates in Japan
Transform · TA Associates · Payroll Inc. · Tokyo, Japan
- **Why now:** TA took the payroll outsourcer private from the Tokyo Stock Exchange in March 2024, so the plan is in its third year. [TA Associates, 19 Mar 24](https://ta.com/news/ta-announces-completion-of-tender-offer-for-payroll-inc)
- **The owner's position:** the business processes payroll for large Japanese enterprises and also sells a cloud payroll platform; moving outsourced clients onto the product is where the margin is.
- **The pitch to TA:** a platform and operations review covering the cloud product's readiness, automation of the outsourced work and what AI changes in payroll processing.
- **Why SSG:** Product and Tech DD and AI Strategy applied after the deal, in a market where the team already works on software holdings.
