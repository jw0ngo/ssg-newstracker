- **Why now:** Morgan Stanley and Jefferies are running a sale of up to 50% of the Vietnamese e-wallet, with Blackstone, CVC and MUFG among the bidders. [Reuters, 3 Jul](https://www.sahmcapital.com/news/content/update-1-blackstone-cvc-and-mufg-among-bidders-for-stake-in-vietnams-momo-sources-say-2026-07-03)
