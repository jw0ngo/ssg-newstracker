Each section's brief is picked from its own feed: relevant stories (the Heat bar), first seen since the previous publish, one entry per story and per situation, the hottest ten at most; on a quiet morning the last 72 hours are read for at least five, marked carried. Commentary, questions and re-told old deals never make it. The angle is written from the event the headline reports (buy, sale, listing, round, fund, hire), the phase it has reached, the region, the size, the sector and, for a portfolio company, the hold clock; it is a prompt for the call, not a summary of the article.
